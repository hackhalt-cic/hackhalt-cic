Privacy Policy

How we collect, process, and protect your personal data

HackHalt Cyber Intelligence Council (HCIC) Last Updated: January 25, 2026

1. Introduction and Scope

The HackHalt Cyber Intelligence Council (HCIC) is a non-profit organization and community movement dedicated to enhancing digital security through collective action and peer-to-peer learning. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of transparency and data protection for our members and users ("Users"). This policy governs the collection, processing, and maintenance of information across all HCIC platforms, including our website, research forums, technical workshops, and communication channels (collectively, the "Service").

2. Information Collection Protocols

HCIC collects data necessary to operate the council, facilitate high-level training, and fulfill its public safety mission.

A. Personal Identification Information (PII)

We collect PII when Users voluntarily engage with our Service through registration, event participation, or expert contributions. This data includes:

  • Identity & Contact Data: Full name, professional email address, and primary contact number.
  • Professional Credentials: Organizational designation (e.g., Student, Security Professional, Mentor, or Subject Matter Expert).
  • Certification & Event Data: Records of attendance and specific identifiers required for professional verification or ISO training initiatives.

B. Technical and Non-Personal Data

To optimize the Service, we automatically collect technical identifiers during User interaction, including:

  • System Information: Device type, operating system, browser specifications, and Internet Service Provider (ISP).
  • Engagement Metrics: Usage patterns, time spent on research platforms, and interaction with community resources.

3. Purpose of Data Processing

HCIC utilizes collected information for the following professional and organizational purposes:

  • Operational Management: To administer user accounts and provide secure access to intelligence forums and discussion groups.
  • Service Optimization: To analyze community trends and tailor technical resources to meet evolving cybersecurity needs.
  • Certification and Training: To verify eligibility for workshops and issue formal certifications or post-event credentials.
  • Strategic Communication: To disseminate security advisories, newsletters, and mission-critical updates.

4. Data Security and Integrity

HCIC adopts rigorous data collection and storage practices. We implement administrative and technical security measures designed to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction of personal data.

5. Third-Party Links and Services

Our Service may contain links to external platforms. HCIC does not control these third-party sites and is not responsible for their privacy practices; Users are encouraged to review the policies of any external entity they visit.
