Refund, Cancellation & Donation Policy

Understanding our non-refundable donation and fee policies

HackHalt Cyber Intelligence Council (HCIC) Section 8 Non-Profit Company Last Updated: January 25, 2026

1. General Non-Refundable Policy

The HackHalt Cyber Intelligence Council (HCIC) operates as a non-profit dedicated to cybercrime prevention and intelligence. To ensure the continuity of our research and community initiatives, HCIC maintains a strict No-Refund and No-Cancellation Policy for all financial contributions, including memberships, service fees, and donations.

2. Donation Policy

  • Finality of Contribution: All donations made to HCIC—whether for specific projects, general funds, or disaster response—are voluntary and strictly non-refundable.
  • Utilization of Funds: Once a donation is processed, the funds are immediately allocated to our four-pillar mission (Prevention, Education, Detection, and Response). As such, we cannot entertain requests for returns or chargebacks.

3. Tax Exemptions and Documentation

  • 12A & 80G Status: HCIC is a registered Section 8 company with Provisional 12A and 80G registration under the Income Tax Act, 1961.
  • Issuance of Receipts: For every donation received, HCIC will issue an official Donation Receipt/Invoice. This document will include our 80G provisional registration number, enabling donors to claim applicable tax deductions as per Indian Law.
  • Delivery of Certificates: Tax-exemption certificates (Form 10BE) will be issued annually at the end of the financial year or as per the statutory timelines defined by the Income Tax Department.
  • Accuracy of Information: It is the donor's responsibility to provide accurate PAN (Permanent Account Number) and contact details at the time of donation to ensure the validity of the tax-exempt receipt.

4. Membership and Service Fees

  • Membership Activation: Once membership is activated or access to the Intelligence Portal is granted, fees are 100% non-refundable.
  • Professional Services: Fees for VAPT, Cyber Investigations, or ISO Training are non-refundable once the engagement has commenced or resources have been deployed.
  • No Pro-rata Refunds: HCIC does not offer partial refunds for unused membership periods or early termination of services by the client.

5. Transaction Errors

In the rare event of a technical error (e.g., a double transaction or an incorrect amount entered by the user), the donor/user must notify HCIC at management@hackhalt.org within 24 hours. HCIC will review the case and, if verified as a technical duplication, may process a correction at its sole discretion, minus any third-party payment gateway charges.

6. Governing Law

This policy is governed by the laws of India. Any disputes arising from transactions or refund requests shall be subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in New Delhi.

7. Administrative Contact

For questions regarding your donation receipt or tax-exempt status, please contact:
